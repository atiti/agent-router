# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentRoute welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or familiarity with coding agents.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and observable behavior.
- Protect credentials, prompts, transcripts, customer data, and other private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety boundaries, and project direction gracefully.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's repository contact options.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens
the community or its users. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with discretion.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
